What it does
Everything your business needs
in one place

No spreadsheets. No paper records. No guesswork. Just clear, accurate data at your fingertips.

📦
Stock Management
Track received and issued stock with batch management and running totals. Instant stock variance alerts when numbers don't add up.
👷
Staff & Earnings
Log daily work, track earnings, deductions and payments per worker. Know exactly what every staff member is owed at any time.
🏪
General Inventory
Manage any product your business carries — fuel, fertiliser, stationery, and more. Admin-defined items, no limits.

Dashboard & Alerts
Live overview of your entire operation. Automatic warning alerts for low stock, unbalanced stock, and pending payments.

Books & Spreadsheets
Built-in spreadsheet editor and record books. Keep financial notes, custom logs and business records — all in the same app.
